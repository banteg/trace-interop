# Nonce-mismatch policy for signed simulation

H13 · Simulation policy proposal · [All decisions](../README.md#decisions-to-review)

**Status: 🔀 Diverging** · [Status definitions](../../decisions/README.md#status-key)

The current draft permits nonce-mismatch simulation. Erigon explicitly favors full transaction validation instead, including nonce, balance and EIP-3607. A mismatched-nonce CREATE fixture is needed to test the execution consequences before settling the policy.

Policy evidence: [Source 1](https://github.com/ethereum/execution-apis/issues/890#issuecomment-5784143408).

[Rule in the pinned draft](https://github.com/banteg/execution-apis/blob/e38fc96c9942f201b683e231f69432729d2737df/docs-api/docs/trace-profile.md#open-details-requiring-focused-review)

## Recommendation

Permit trace_rawTransaction simulation despite a mismatch between the signed nonce and the sender’s state nonce. This does not relax signature, chain identity, funds, intrinsic gas or fee checks; their policies remain separate. Simulation success does not establish block-inclusion validity.

Besu, Erigon and Nethermind execute the high-nonce transfer; Reth and the tested Geth draft reject it. Acceptance alone does not demonstrate rewriting the signed nonce. The transfer fixture does not discriminate nonce effects on CREATE addresses. This proposal favors compatibility with observed simulation behavior, rather than treating majority behavior as proof of correctness.
