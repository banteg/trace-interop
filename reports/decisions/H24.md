# H24 — Sibling failure isolation

**Disposition:** Reproduced reporting defect. Proposal, awaiting client review.

## Proposed behavior

Attach error and result data to the actual call frame. A sibling failure must not change another frame's status.

## Rationale

A successful caller can handle a failed subcall and continue; frame results must preserve that distinction.

## September 15 observation

For CALL(revert) followed by CALL(return42), Besu marks the successful second sibling Reverted and omits its result. Reversing sibling order avoids the error. All other Parity traces and every debug callTracer, including Besu, identify the second call as successful with return word 42. The failing order was repeated.

## Unresolved review

Prepare a focused Besu regression and fix without waiting for broad schema policy.
