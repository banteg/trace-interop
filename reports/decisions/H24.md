# Sibling failure isolation

H24 · Reproduced reporting defect · [All decisions](../README.md#decisions-to-review)

**Status: ⚪ Under review** · [Status definitions](../../decisions/README.md#status-key)

No policy conclusion has been recorded. Implementation observations below do not establish client-team agreement.

## Recommendation

Attach error and result data to the actual call frame. A sibling failure must not change another frame's status.

A successful caller can handle a failed subcall and continue; frame results must preserve that distinction.
