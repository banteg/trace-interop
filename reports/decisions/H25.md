# Well-formed errors for rejected raw transactions

H25 · Reproduced response serialization defect · [All decisions](../README.md#decisions-to-review)

**Status: ⚪ Under review** · [Status definitions](../../decisions/README.md#status-key)

No policy conclusion has been recorded. Implementation observations below do not establish client-team agreement.

## Recommendation

Return one complete valid JSON-RPC error response on validation failure. Do not begin serializing a result before validation has succeeded.

Clients must be able to parse failures and associate them with a request, regardless of the chosen signed-transaction admission policy.
