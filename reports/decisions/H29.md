# H29 — Precompile call-frame inclusion

**Disposition:** Spec decision / client alignment. Proposal, awaiting client review.

## Proposed behavior

Retain root precompile calls. Omit nested precompile frames with zero value; retain those with nonzero transferred or inherited value, whether successful or failed. Number traceAddress and subtraces over the emitted tree. Keep caller VM return-memory effects even when a child frame is omitted.

## Rationale

All four tested clients omit nested zero-value precompiles. Erigon, Reth and Nethermind retain nonzero-value CALL, CALLCODE and DELEGATECALL frames; Besu omits them. Preserving this shared exception avoids changing existing tree paths and retains the value-bearing action context.

## Open questions

Review the nonzero-value exception with Besu and confirm it for additional precompiles and fork activations. The precompiles corpus uses BN254 addition, with valid and invalid inputs, across CALL, STATICCALL, CALLCODE and DELEGATECALL. Frame inclusion is separate from failure propagation and return-byte correctness.
